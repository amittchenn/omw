Common questions

How do I add friends? Open your profile (Me tab) and share your friend ID, or type a friend's ID.

Why does omw! want my location? To work out how long it takes you to get to a hangout and tell you when to leave. You can turn off sharing your live location in your profile.

Notifications or the Dynamic Island aren't showing. Check Settings → omw! → Notifications, and Settings → omw! → Live Activities.

How do I report or block someone? Tap ⋯ on their post in the feed.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Me → Delete account. Everything is deleted right away.
