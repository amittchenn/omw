Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

omw! helps friends plan hangouts and show up on time. This page explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and profile picture (from Google or Apple if you sign in with them, or the ones you enter), and the friend ID we give you.
  • Your friends: who you're friends with and your friend requests.
  • Plans: hangouts you make or join: the place, time, who's invited, who's going, when each person arrived, and the group chat.
  • Location: your home location if you set it, and your current location while the app is open, used to work out travel times and when you need to leave. Around hangout time, your location is shared with the people at that hangout so they can see you're on your way. You can stop sharing your live location in your profile at any time.
  • Photos: group photos you post. You choose whether a photo is shared with your friends' feeds.
  • Messages: direct messages and group chats with friends.
  • Your schedule: busy times you type in, or that you choose to bring in from Google Calendar, so we can find times everyone is free.
  • Check-ins: how early or late you arrive, used for the leaderboard and to make your leave-now alerts more accurate.
  • Device: a notification token so we can send you alerts, if you allow notifications.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Who can see it

Your profile, status (free, on your way, out) and check-in record are visible to your friends. Plans, their chat and live locations are visible to the people in that plan. Group photos are visible to the people who were there and, if the poster chose to share it, to their friends.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, the database that stores everything above, and notifications.
  • Google Maps Platform and Mapbox: maps, place search and travel times. They receive the places and coordinates needed for each search or route.
  • OpenAI and Meta: when you type a plan in plain words, make an AI avatar or ask for a fair spot, that text (and the names of the friends involved) is sent to the AI model to understand it. They don't receive your location history or messages.
  • Our hosting provider runs the omw! server.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, picture, friends list, location, your posts, your comments, likes and ratings, and every message you sent. It can't be undone. Removing a single post is in its menu.

Things you shared with other people stay theirs, without you: a plan you made that others are going to passes to one of them; a group photo with other people in it stays for them; a one-on-one chat stays for the other person (without your messages), showing you as "Deleted user".

Children

omw! is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
